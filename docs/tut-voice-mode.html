Talk: voice conversations

Speak to the assistant and hear the answer. A spoken request uses the same tools, workspace, and approvals as a typed request. Thus, a spoken "pull the branch" gets the same checks as a typed one.

Talk is a conversation, not dictation. You speak, the assistant answers with speech, and the assistant does the work that you ask for. Examples are to read files, run commands, list branches, and write documents. Dictation and meeting recording are different features, and their audio stays on this Mac. Refer to Voice & meetings.

Your audio goes out of this Mac. During a voice conversation, the app sends your microphone audio to Amazon Bedrock. The spoken answers return from Amazon Bedrock. Dictation and meeting recording do not send audio. When the conversation is off, the app sends no audio.

Before you start

  1. Select Hybrid or Cloud mode

    Talk works only in the Hybrid and Cloud model modes. A new install starts in Local mode. In Local mode, the "Talk" button is disabled. Open "Settings", then "Model mode". In the "Mode" menu, select "Hybrid (pick per capability)" or "Cloud (all Bedrock)". Refer to Model modes.

  2. Get access to the model in Amazon Bedrock

    Your AWS account must have access to amazon.nova-2-sonic-v1:0 in the voice region. The voice region is voice.region. When voice.region is empty (the default), the app uses bedrock_region. The default of bedrock_region is us-east-1.

    Nova 2 Sonic is available only in us-east-1, us-west-2, eu-north-1, and ap-northeast-1. If your chat region is not in this list, set voice.region to one of these regions.

  3. Examine your credentials

    Talk uses the same AWS credentials as the chat: aws configure, AWS_PROFILE, or SSO. Talk does not accept a Bedrock API key (AWS_BEARER_TOKEN_BEDROCK), because this API accepts only signed requests.

  4. Give the microphone permission

    At the first conversation, the browser or macOS asks for the microphone. Click "Allow". Talk opens the microphone with echo cancellation, because the answer comes from the same speakers. In a room with noise, use headphones.

Start and stop a conversation

The "Talk" button is the small equalizer icon in the chat box, between the dictation microphone and "Send". Its tooltip is "Talk with the assistant hands-free. Your audio goes to Amazon Bedrock (Nova 2 Sonic) while this is on."

  1. Click "Talk"

    The voice bar replaces the chat box until the conversation stops.

  2. Speak

    Speak in your usual voice. The assistant answers when you stop for a short time.

  3. Stop the conversation

    Click the square button on the right of the voice bar. Its tooltip is "End voice conversation". You can also say "stop" or "goodbye". The assistant then says a short goodbye and stops the conversation.

Work continues after the conversation stops. Requests that run continue, and their answers come into the chat. To stop them also, click the "Stop" button. "Stop" replaces "Send" while background work runs.

How it works

Two models share the conversation:

  • Amazon Nova 2 Sonic is the voice. It receives your speech and answers with speech over one bidirectional stream on Amazon Bedrock. It answers greetings itself, and its answers are one or two sentences. It does not read lists, paths, file names, or command output aloud.
  • The chat model in the toolbar does the work. Nova 2 Sonic sends each work request to the chat model as one written request. The chat model runs a usual turn with all the tools, the connected workspace, your permission mode, and the approvals of this session.

The work runs on a cloud chat model. If you select an on-device model, the app refuses the spoken work request. The message tells you to select a cloud model in the model picker. The app does not change to a different model.

Thus, a spoken request and a typed request get the same tools, approval cards, cost records, and agents. Nova 2 Sonic only sends your words and speaks a short form of the answer.

You can speak at any time. When you speak, the spoken answer stops. If you speak while the assistant works, Nova 2 Sonic continues to listen. If you spell a name, the request keeps the name. For example, "m l dash o p s" becomes ml-ops. Before a change or a delete, the assistant says the exact operation and asks you, because speech recognition can confuse "pull" with "push".

What the chat shows

The conversation writes usual chat messages into the session where it started. You can scroll, copy, and export them.

  • One bubble for each turn. Your speech and the answer of the assistant show as different bubbles. The app joins the parts of your speech into one bubble for each turn. The words and the text of the assistant until you speak again show in one bubble. A bubble shows the mark "spoken" next to its time when a part of it was spoken.
  • The written answer is in the same bubble. You hear a short spoken summary. The full written answer shows in the same bubble, with its tool activity, agent rows, and team report. The assistant does not speak the written answer.
  • A live bubble during the work. The bubble shows the words that Nova 2 Sonic speaks now, with the label "Speaking" or "Thinking", and the steps that run.

Approvals and questions

When the assistant must ask you, its turn stops, as in a typed chat. Nova 2 Sonic asks you the question in one sentence. At the same time, the usual card shows in the chat:

CardAnswer with your voiceAnswer with a click
Approval needed (with the risk badge, the command, or the path)Say yes or no"Yes" or "No"
Question for youSay your answerOne button for each option
Folder neededSay the folder name or the path-

The two methods answer the same request one time only. A spoken "yes" and a click at the same time do not run the action two times. After your answer, the turn continues.

A "no" word always wins. If your answer contains a refusal word, the app reads it as no. For example, "okay, no" and "yes but don't" are both no. An approval must contain a clear approval word. A sound like "uh huh" during the work is not an approval. Refer to Permissions & approvals.

Long tasks continue in the background

The answer to a quick request returns while you wait. If a request runs for more than 20 s, it continues in the background. Nova 2 Sonic tells you this, and you can ask for more work. Maximum 3 requests can run at the same time.

  • If you stop the conversation while a request runs, the chat toolbar shows a "Working in background" chip. "Stop" replaces "Send" until the request completes.
  • When the result returns, Nova 2 Sonic speaks a short summary. The full written answer shows in the chat.
  • Ask "what is it doing?" to hear the requests that run, their time, and their agents.
  • A request has no time limit, as in a typed chat. It stops at the round limit in "Settings", "Costs", "Budget". "Stop" stops it before that limit.
  • A request in the background can also stop for an approval. Answer the card with your voice during the conversation, or with a click after the conversation.

Open a folder by its name

Say "open the ml-ops folder in Documents". The assistant searches for the name, and it does not make a path from the words. The search ignores uppercase, lowercase, and punctuation. Thus, ml-ops, ML_OPS, and "m l dash o p s" find the same folder.

The search looks in the folder that you name, in the usual home folders (Documents, Desktop, Downloads, Developer, and Projects), and in the parent folders of recent workspaces. If one folder matches, the assistant opens it. If more folders match, the assistant tells you their names and asks you. If no folder matches, the assistant tells you. A workspace that opens this way is the same as a workspace from the workspace picker.

Type during a conversation

Click "Type instead (voice stays on)" in the voice bar, or the "Voice on" chip in the toolbar. The text box shows again, and the conversation continues. The conversation reads your text as speech. Thus, you can paste an exact command or a long path. To go back to the voice bar, click the chip again.

Record a meeting by voice. Ask the assistant to start or stop the meeting recording. The assistant then uses the "Record" button. The app makes the transcript on this Mac.

The window has no voice picker. To change the voice, edit config.user.json:

  1. Open the configuration editor

    Open "Settings", then "Models", then "Chat". Click the "config.user.json" link. You can also open the file in a text editor. The file is in ~/.whisper for the Mac app, and in the checkout for a source install.

  2. Set the voice

    Set voice.voice_id, for example to "matthew".

  3. Optional: set the pause length

    Set voice.endpointing to LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH. This value sets how fast the voice model ends your turn after a pause.

  4. Save the file

    The change applies to the next conversation.

What goes out of this Mac

During the conversation, your microphone audio goes to Amazon Bedrock. The spoken answers and the text of the conversation return from Amazon Bedrock. The work runs on your chat model, and the tools read your files on this Mac. Meeting recording and dictation do not send audio. When you stop the conversation, the app closes the stream.
