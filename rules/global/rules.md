# Global rules

Rules in this folder apply in every project and every chat. One rule per line.
The assistant adds a line here when you say "always", "everywhere" or "all the
time"; project-only rules go to rules/<project>/rules.md next to this folder.

- Do not use emojis or emoticons unless I explicitly ask for them.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes. Prefer a comma, period, parentheses, a colon, or a short spaced hyphen.
